## 📌 Executive Summary

I am a **Full-Stack & AI/ML Engineer** with a passion for transforming complex raw data and AI research into high-performance, containerized production software. Currently interning as a **Data Science Intern at Amdox Technologies** and pursuing my BCA at **PSIT Kanpur**.
